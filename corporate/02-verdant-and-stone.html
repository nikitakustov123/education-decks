01 · The studio
Forty-two people who believe a building should give back more than it takes.

Since 2011, our architects, landscape designers and builders have worked side by side — so the garden is never an afterthought, and the building is never a box.

Verdant & StoneEst. 2011
02 · Philosophy
Three roots, one practice.
Rooted

We start with the site — its sun path, soil, water and neighbors — before we draw a single wall.

Living

Daylight, fresh air and plants in every room. People work, heal and learn better in buildings that breathe.

Lasting

Timber, stone and lime chosen to age beautifully — and designed to stand for the next hundred years.
